# Delete

Deletes a record:

1. In the listing, open the menu of the row (**⋯**) of the record — or open
   the record and its menu.
2. Choose **Delete**, and confirm.

Where the listing has a **Trash** tab, the record is not gone: it goes there,
with who deleted it and when, and may be brought back —
see {%= admin.doc("actions/restore").link %} and
{%= admin.doc("actions/deleted_origin").link %}. Elsewhere, the deletion is
for good.

A record others depend on may not be deleted while they do.

## When it is available

To whoever may delete the record, in a listing that deletes records.
