# Deleted from

Shows where a record of the trash was deleted from: who deleted it, when, the
address and the browser of the request, and — when the address could be
located — the place, on a map.

Open it from the menu of the row (**⋯**) of a record in the **Trash** tab of a
listing.

To bring the record back, see {%= admin.doc("actions/restore").link %}.

## When it is available

Only for a deleted record: in the Trash tab of its listing, or in its detail opened from there.
