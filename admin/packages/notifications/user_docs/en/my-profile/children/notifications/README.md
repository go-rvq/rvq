# Notifications

A **notification** is something you are told: a transfer left a wallet below
zero, an invite came… It is kept until you read it.

- In the **bell**, at the top of the screen, are the newest **unread** ones:
  a click opens what it is about and marks it read; **Mark all as read**
  marks them all; **See all** opens this page.
- On your **profile** (the dashboard at its top), the **Notifications**
  widget shows the unread ones; the button of each one marks it read.
- On this page, in **My profile → Notifications**, are all of yours — yours
  only: nobody sees another's.

## The tabs

- **Unread** (the one it opens on), **Read** (with **Read at**, when you read
  it) and **All**.

## Read or unread

In the column of the actions, at the left of the **…** menu, an icon marks
the notification **read** (the open envelope) or **unread** again; it asks to
confirm.

## Sending a notification

**Send notification**, at the top of the list, sends a notification to
other users: choose **To** whom (one or more), write its **title**, its
**text** and its **level** (information, warning, error). They see it as you
wrote it — it is not translated —, with the origin **Users**. Only who may
see the list of users has it.

## The filters

- **When**: from a date and time to another.
- **Period**: today, the last 7, 30, 90 or 365 days (today among them).
- **Origin**: **System** — what the system tells —, **Users** — what a user
  sent — or **Others**.

## System or user

The notifications of the **system** (the **System** field checked) are
shown in **your language**: their title and text are translated when you read
them. What a **user** sends is shown as they wrote it. The **Source** says
who made it.

## Deleting

Deleting a notification sends it to the **Trash** of this page, where it can
be restored.
