# People

> Who is registered in the organization?

## What it shows

- How many active people the organization has.
- The five registered last, with their document; each one opens their record.
- **See all** opens the list of the people of the organization.

## Where the data comes from

The people registry of the organization (the deleted ones left out).

## Who sees it

Whoever may list the people. On the Dashboard of each organization; the data is of it alone.

## Details

- Group: **Organization**.
- Its id, in the preferences of each user: `orgs/people`.
