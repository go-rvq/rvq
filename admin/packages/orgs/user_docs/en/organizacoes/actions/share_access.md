# Share

Gives a **user or group** a **role** on the organization — and on what is
under it. Choose them and the role, then save.

The sharing waits for the **owner**: they get an e-mail to confirm it.
Confirmed, a user gets an invite in their notifications, to accept; a
group's members reach the organization at once.

Shares the organization whoever is its owner or has its **Administrator**
role.
