# Accesses

Who reaches the organization: its **owner** first, then each user and group
it is shared with, and their role.

- Change a **role** in its list: a role that may more asks the owner by
  e-mail, as a new sharing; one that may less is at once.
- **Revoke** (the icon beside the role) takes the access away at once.

An access that comes from above — a record under the organization reached by
the organization's — says **inherited**, and is changed where it comes from.
