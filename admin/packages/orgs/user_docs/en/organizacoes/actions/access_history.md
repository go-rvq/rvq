# Access history

Everything that happened to the sharing of the organization, newest first,
kept forever: when, what, to whom, the role (before → after, when it
changed) and by whom. Among the events:

- **confirmation asked to the owner**, **confirmed by the owner**,
  **declined by the owner**, **confirmation expired** — the e-mail to the
  owner and its answer;
- **invited**, **invite accepted**, **invite declined** — the invite of a
  user;
- **access granted**, **role changed**, **access revoked**;
- **ownership transfer asked**, **ownership transferred**;
- **joined the group**, **left the group** — a member of a group that has
  access.
