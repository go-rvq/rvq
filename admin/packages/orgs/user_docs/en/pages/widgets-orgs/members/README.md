# Members

> Who is the organization shared with?

## What it shows

- How many users and groups reach the organization.
- Each one, with their role (Viewer, Editor…), the organization's first.

## Where the data comes from

The accesses of the sharing of the organization.

## Who sees it

With the organizations shared. On the [Dashboard](../../../organizacoes/pages/painel.md) of each organization, by whom reaches it; the data is of it alone.

## Details

- Group: **Organization**.
- Its id, in the preferences of each user: `orgs/members`.
