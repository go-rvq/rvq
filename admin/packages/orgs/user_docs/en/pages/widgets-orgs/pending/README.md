# Pending requests and invites

> Is a sharing waiting?

## What it shows

- The requests waiting for the owner to confirm them by e-mail (marked orange).
- The invites waiting for whom was invited to accept them (blue).
- The newest first, with the date.

## Where the data comes from

The requests and invites of the sharing of the organization.

## Who sees it

With the organizations shared. On the [Dashboard](../../../organizacoes/pages/painel.md) of each organization, by whom reaches it; the data is of it alone.

## Details

- Group: **Organization**.
- Its id, in the preferences of each user: `orgs/pending`.
