# The top bar and the accounts

At the top of every screen of the admin is the **top bar**. From left to
right:

- **≡** opens and closes the side menu;
- the **brand** of the site (its logo): a click goes back to the Dashboard;
- **Search the admin** (on a phone, the magnifier): see below;
- **Visit website** (the arrow pointing out): opens the site in a new tab;
- **?** opens and closes the [help bar](../help-bar/README.md) (the **F1** key too);
- the **bell** shows the notifications;
- the **user button**: your picture (or your initials), your name and your
  role. It opens the **user menu**.

## Searching the admin

Click **Search the admin**, or press **Ctrl K** (**⌘ K** on a Mac) on any
screen, and type at least two letters. There show:

- the **pages of the menu** whose name has what you typed (accents and case
  aside);
- the **records** that have what you typed, up to five of each kind (pages,
  posts, messages…), of the kinds your role may see alone.

The **↑** and **↓** arrows choose, **Enter** opens, **Esc** closes. A click
opens too.

## The side menu: whole or its icons alone

At the foot of the side menu, **Collapse menu** leaves the menu with the
icons of its groups alone, and the screen gains room. Hover an icon: the
group opens in a floating menu, with its items. An item with no group shows
its name on hover. **Expand menu** brings the whole menu back. The browser
remembers your choice. On a phone the menu is always the whole one.

## The user menu

- **Your account**: the name, the e-mail and the role. A click on the
  picture opens your profile.
- **Quick access**: My profile, My access keys, Login sessions (the places
  where you are logged in), Notifications (with how many are unread) and My
  activity (what you did in the admin), as your role allows.
- The **language** of the admin.
- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **Auto** (your system's, following it
  when it changes). The browser remembers the choice.
- **Log in with another account** and **Log out**.

To change your picture, open **My profile**, click edit and choose the
**Picture** in the media library (or upload one). It is cropped square.

## Several accounts in the same browser

As in Gmail, you may stay logged in with more than one account at once (up
to 5):

1. in the user menu, **Log in with another account** opens the login, titled
   *Log in with another account*. The accounts already logged in stay logged
   in, and **Back** takes you back to one of them;
2. the new account opens at an address with its number: `/admin/u/1/…`. The
   first account stays at `/admin/…`, as ever;
3. the user menu of each account shows the **Other accounts**: a click on one
   opens the same page in it. When that account may not see the page, its
   Dashboard opens, with a notice.

Each tab of the browser may be in an account. The green dot on the user
button shows how many other accounts are logged in.

- **Log out of this account** logs out the account of the screen alone; the
  browser goes to another account logged in.
- **Log out of all accounts** logs out all of them.
- An account whose session **expired** shows faded in the list, with *log in
  again*: the login comes with its e-mail already in.

### On the site

The user bar at the top of the site follows the account of the last admin
you opened. With several accounts, your name opens the menu of the accounts:
a click on one shows the page of the site in it. The links to the admin, the
profile, *edit this page* and log out go to the account of the site.
