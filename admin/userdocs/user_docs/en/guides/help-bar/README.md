# Help bar

At the right of every screen of the admin is the **help bar**: the
documentation of what you are doing, without leaving the screen. It takes a
strip of its own and the screen narrows beside it, without being covered.

## Opening and closing it

- Closed, the bar is only a narrow strip with the help icon (**?**). Click
  it to open the bar.
- Open, the **»** button at its top closes it again.
- The **F1** key opens and closes the bar on any screen.
- Drag the left edge of the bar to change its width; a double click on the
  edge brings the initial width back.

The browser remembers whether the bar was open, and its width.

## The Focus tab

The first tab, **Focus**, always shows the help of what is in focus, and
changes by itself as you work:

1. with a **dialog** open (a form, a preview), its help;
2. otherwise, the help of the **drawer** (a record open, its edit) or of the
   **page** you clicked last;
3. when a dialog or a drawer closes, it goes back to the help of what is left
   on the screen.

Beside the name of the tab it says where the help comes from: *Page*,
*Drawer* or *Dialog*.

## Reference tabs

A link to another document, clicked in the Focus tab, opens in a **new tab**
of the bar; so do the help buttons of the fields (the **?** beside a field,
the button of the language of a code field, such as **Gadx**). A document
that already has a tab open goes back to it, without repeating it.

In a reference tab:

- its links take you to other documents in the same tab;
- the **←** and **→** buttons at the top go back and forward among the
  documents the tab has shown;
- the **X** of the tab closes it.

Only the Focus tab follows the screen; the others stay on the document you
opened. With many tabs, their bar scrolls sideways.

## The other buttons

- **Open in the documentation**: opens the document of the current tab in
  the Documentation page, in a tab of the browser.
- **Reload**: shows the document of the current tab again.

With **Ctrl** (or **⌘**) held, a documentation link opens in a tab of the
browser, like any link.

On the Documentation page itself the bar is not shown.
