# Publishing

A record that goes on the site — a page, a post — is edited as a **draft**:
what is saved is not shown on the site until it is **published**.

- **Publish** puts the record, as it is now, on the site.
- **Unpublish** takes it off the site; the draft is kept.
- **Republish** puts the changes made since the last publication on the site.
- **Schedule** publishes (or unpublishes) it at a date and time.

The status of the record is shown at the top of its detail: *Draft*,
*Online*, *Offline*. Several records are published at once from their
listing: select them and choose **Publish** among the bulk actions.
